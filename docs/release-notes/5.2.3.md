# PhoneKeep 5.2.3

File search, a timeline to go back through your backups, chats shown as on the phone, call recordings and saves protected with a password. Everything in [the 5.2.2 notes](https://github.com/aminyoutb007-hash/PhoneKeep/blob/main/docs/release-notes/5.2.2.md) is still true, plus what's below.

**Verified on a Mac (Apple Silicon) with a real iPhone. The Windows check is deferred:** every automated test runs on Windows on each change, but no check with a real iPhone on a Windows PC was done for this release.

## New in 5.2.3

- **Search for files.** In *All files*, the search box takes a name or a pattern: `*` matches anything and `?` one character (`*.pdf`, `invoice*`). Case and accents don't matter. In *App Files*, one search goes through every app that shares files on the iPhone. Results show where each file is, its size and date; preview, open or save them.
- **Backup timeline.** Once a phone has two snapshots or more, a slider in the Snapshot card goes from the oldest to the latest. Drag it, click it or use the arrow keys: the window shows the phone as it was then within about 2 seconds, with the screen you had open, and "Viewing …" on the phone picture.
- **Chats as on the phone.** In Messages, WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business, voice notes show a waveform and their length and play with a click, and links show a preview card. In Messages, replies also show the message they quote, and reactions sit beside the message. The card is made from what the apps already kept in the backup: nothing is fetched online, and the card opens nothing.
- **Call recordings.** Calls recorded on the iPhone (iOS 18.1 and later) are listed in the phone window: who, when, how long, and the iPhone's transcript when it made one. Play them, or save the audio and transcript. They're in *Save everything* too.
- **Saves protected with a password.** A *Password* switch in the phone window's header protects what you save from the phone's data: chats, notes, files, lists, media and *Save everything*. A save that makes only PDFs (chats or notes saved as PDF) gives password-protected PDFs, which open in current PDF readers. Anything else goes into one encrypted ZIP (AES-256), which needs Keka or The Unarchiver on a Mac, or 7-Zip on Windows: the Mac's and Windows' own ZIP tools can't open it. PhoneKeep doesn't keep the password, so without it the files can't be opened.

## Fixed

- **Tamper check: a seal record could lose its entries.** If the record of sealed backups couldn't be read for a moment, it could be written back with only the newest entry, and earlier backups then showed "can't check". Now PhoneKeep tries again and writes nothing if it still can't read it. A damaged record is kept aside and a new one started.

## Known limits

- Everything in [the 5.2.2 notes' Known limits](https://github.com/aminyoutb007-hash/PhoneKeep/blob/main/docs/release-notes/5.2.2.md#known-limits) is still true.
- Encrypted ZIPs: the names of the files inside can be seen without the password (only their contents are locked). Opening a protected save on a Windows PC hasn't been tried by hand yet.
- Call recordings: a real transcript hasn't been seen yet (the iPhone used for checking made none); transcripts are checked with test data.
- Chats: the new look hasn't been compared side by side with the phone yet. WhatsApp chats don't show replies or reactions. Voice notes in Messages haven't been tried on a real backup yet (WhatsApp's have).
- Saves with a password: Clean up's "save before deleting", Print, and saving the iPhone's details, battery history or activity log aren't protected.
- File search: at most 5,000 results are shown (the count says how many in all). In *App Files*, very large apps are searched only partly (up to 20,000 entries, 30 folders deep), without saying so yet.
