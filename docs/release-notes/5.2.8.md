# PhoneKeep 5.2.8

The storage map's Photos now match the Photos app, and a few safety fixes for when a phone window closes while it's reading. Everything in [the 5.2.7 notes](https://github.com/aminyoutb007-hash/PhoneKeep/blob/main/docs/release-notes/5.2.7.md) is still true, plus what's below.

**Verified on a Mac (Apple Silicon) with a real iPhone. The Windows check is deferred:** every automated test runs on Windows on each change, but no check with a real iPhone on a Windows PC was done for this release.

## Fixed

- **The storage map's Photos count everything the Photos app keeps.** With iCloud Photos, the photos and videos the iPhone has downloaded back from iCloud sit in a different folder from the camera's own. The map and the Storage screen counted only the camera's folder. Now they count both.
- **Recently Deleted on the storage map.** Photos and videos in the Photos app's Recently Deleted still take space until the iPhone empties it. The map's Photos level now shows them as their own block, "Recently Deleted", and says that. It shows only what the Photos library marks as deleted.
- **The map's videos are the ones the Photos app lists.** A Live Photo's moving part is part of the photo, not a separate video, as in the Photos app and Photo clean-up. A video file the Photos app doesn't list counts as other files.
- **Closing a phone window while it saves a photo.** Saving a photo that was already copied out of the backup could fail with "file not found" if the window closed at that moment. Now the save finishes first.
- **Smaller safety fixes when the phone window reads a backup:** the backup's photo list is made once, even when two parts of the window ask for it at once; the Apps list reads its file counts with the backup held open.
- **The tamper check's record forgets deleted snapshots.** When PhoneKeep deletes a snapshot (Delete… or the automatic clean-up), its entry leaves the record of seals, so the record no longer grows for ever. A snapshot that is only missing (its drive unplugged) keeps its entry.

## Known limits

- Everything in [the 5.2.7 notes' Known limits](https://github.com/aminyoutb007-hash/PhoneKeep/blob/main/docs/release-notes/5.2.7.md#known-limits) is still true.
- Save everything's estimate of the space your photos need still counts only the camera's folder, not the photos downloaded back from iCloud.
