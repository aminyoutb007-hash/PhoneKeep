# PhoneKeep 5.2.9

Save everything's size estimate now counts the photos it really saves, and the storage map pairs Live Photos the way the Photos app does. Everything in [the 5.2.8 notes](https://github.com/aminyoutb007-hash/PhoneKeep/blob/main/docs/release-notes/5.2.8.md) is still true, plus what's below.

**Verified on a Mac (Apple Silicon) with a real iPhone. The Windows check is deferred:** every automated test runs on Windows on each change, but no check with a real iPhone on a Windows PC was done for this release.

## Fixed

- **Save everything's estimate counts the photos it saves.** The estimate of the space your photos need counted every file in the camera's folder. It missed the photos and videos downloaded back from iCloud, and counted photos in Recently Deleted and edit files, which Save everything doesn't save. Now it adds up exactly the photos and videos Save everything copies, with their Live Photo videos. The sheet says "Working out the size…" until it's ready.
- **The storage map pairs Live Photos the way the Photos app does.** It used to decide which video belongs to a Live Photo by file name alone. Now it goes by the Photos library's own record of each Live Photo. A video that is a video in its own right stays a video, even next to a photo of the same name. A Live Photo's video counts with its photo even when the photo itself is kept only in iCloud. The file name is used only when the library says nothing about a video, and only when exactly one photo has that name.
- **The storage map's blocks fit the map exactly.** With sizes very far apart, the last blocks could end a fraction of a pixel short of the map's edge or past it. Blocks under 2 pixels across were drawn about 2 pixels wide, over their neighbours. Now they are drawn at their true size.

## Known limits

- Everything in [the 5.2.7 notes' Known limits](https://github.com/aminyoutb007-hash/PhoneKeep/blob/main/docs/release-notes/5.2.7.md#known-limits) is still true. (5.2.8's limit on Save everything's estimate is fixed above.)
- Save everything saves a Live Photo that was downloaded back from iCloud as its photo only, without its moving part.
