# PhoneKeep 5.2.6

Contact cards from group chats, and numbers for WhatsApp group members whose numbers were hidden. Everything in [the 5.2.5 notes](https://github.com/aminyoutb007-hash/PhoneKeep/blob/main/docs/release-notes/5.2.5.md) is still true, plus what's below.

**Verified on a Mac (Apple Silicon) with a real iPhone. The Windows check is deferred:** every automated test runs on Windows on each change, but no check with a real iPhone on a Windows PC was done for this release.

## New in 5.2.6

- **Contact cards from a group chat.** In a group chat's People window, **Contact card** opens the chosen person's card. When no card can be found, the window says why: it's you, they aren't in the iPhone's Contacts, or the app didn't keep their number.
- **WhatsApp group members with hidden numbers get their numbers.** Newer WhatsApp hides group members' numbers behind ids of its own. PhoneKeep now reads, from the backup, which number each id belongs to. So those members now have their numbers in the People window, in the Group sender number column of the full-detail Excel/CSV, and in the group chats on their contact cards. Before, they were left out.

## Changed

- **A number that two contacts share** now says so ("is on 2 contacts in the iPhone, so PhoneKeep can't tell whose card it is") instead of saying it isn't in Contacts, both in a group chat's People window and from Calls and Voicemail.
- The library PhoneKeep uses to talk to the iPhone (pymobiledevice3) is a newer version, checked with a real iPhone.

## Known limits

- Everything in [the 5.2.5 notes' Known limits](https://github.com/aminyoutb007-hash/PhoneKeep/blob/main/docs/release-notes/5.2.5.md#known-limits) is still true, except that the people in a group chat now open their contact cards.
- Contact cards: long lines in a card's lists are cut at its edge.
