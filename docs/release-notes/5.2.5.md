# PhoneKeep 5.2.5

Contact cards with everything about a person, a storage map, and a faster timeline. Everything in [the 5.2.4 notes](https://github.com/aminyoutb007-hash/PhoneKeep/blob/main/docs/release-notes/5.2.4.md) is still true, plus what's below.

**Verified on a Mac (Apple Silicon) with a real iPhone. The Windows check is deferred:** every automated test runs on Windows on each change, but no check with a real iPhone on a Windows PC was done for this release.

## New in 5.2.5

- **Contact cards with everything about a person.** In Contacts, a person's card now has Chats, Calls and Voicemail tabs at its foot, newest first:
  - their chats in Messages, WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business, and group chats they wrote in;
  - their calls, including FaceTime by email;
  - the voicemails they left.

  Click a chat to open it, or a call or voicemail to find it chosen in its list. The other way round, a one-to-one chat, Calls and Voicemail have a **Contact card** button. A number written differently ("+44 7700 900123" and "07700 900123") is the same person. A number two contacts share opens neither card, since whose it is can't be told.
- **Storage map.** In Storage, **Storage map ›** shows what fills the iPhone as coloured blocks, each as big as the space it takes:
  - **Photos** opens into videos, photos and other files. Videos shows the largest videos, and a click on one opens Photo clean-up on Large videos, where they can be saved to the computer.
  - **Apps** shows the largest apps.
  - **Messages** shows its attachments and the messages themselves.

  The path at the top goes back up. As on the Storage screen, the sizes are estimates: inside Photos and Messages they come from the latest backup, and inside Apps from what the iPhone reports for each app.

## Fixed

- **Going back to the latest snapshot on the timeline could take about 14 seconds** after you'd looked at other screens, such as Photos and Contacts. Now it takes about a second, like going back.

## Known limits

- Everything in [the 5.2.4 notes' Known limits](https://github.com/aminyoutb007-hash/PhoneKeep/blob/main/docs/release-notes/5.2.4.md#known-limits) is still true.
- Contact cards: the people in a group chat (its People window) don't open their cards yet. Long lines in a card's lists are cut at its edge.
- Storage map: photos kept only in iCloud aren't counted, as on the Storage screen. A click on one video opens the Large videos list, not that video. When the iPhone's System space can't be estimated (the app sizes it reports overlap with it), System isn't on the map, and the map says so.
